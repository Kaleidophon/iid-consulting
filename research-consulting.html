Research Consulting

Finding signal in the noise.

Get in touch
← Back to Services

Even in the age of AI-based research assistants, getting an understanding of available solutions to a problem isn't easy. Academic papers can overplay results, and existing resources made accessible for a broader audience can miss solutions to domain-specific problems. In addition, the field moves at a breakneck speed - new ideas can become obsolete within the span of a year. Years of research expertise can help to find the solutions that will work in practice.

What this service covers

Research consulting brings academic rigor to problems in AI and NLP that don't have easy off-the-shelf answers. This includes problem formulation, systematic literature review, experimental design, and the kind of careful evaluation that identifies subtle problems and opportunities for improvement.

Engagements range from focused one-off analyses to extended research collaborations - whatever scope fits the problem.

Who is this for?

Teams without dedicated research capacity who need a credible external perspective. Startups assessing technical feasibility before committing resources. Academic groups looking for a collaborator with both research and industry experience.

What you get

Clear, written deliverables tailored to the question: reports, literature surveys, evaluation frameworks, or experimental designs. Where relevant, working code and data analysis pipelines. The output will always provide actionable insights.
